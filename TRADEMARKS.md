## Forks and redistributions

You're welcome to fork VesperTV. If you distribute a modified version, or any build that isn't an
official release from https://github.com/NoXqc/VesperTV or https://vespertv.app:

- give it a **different name** and **your own icon and logo**;
- don't present it as VesperTV, as an official or endorsed version, or as made by the VesperTV
  project;
- run **your own pairing/transfer relay** (`tools/pair_relay/`) — `pair.vespertv.app` serves the
  official app only.

You may still say truthfully that your app is "based on VesperTV" or "a fork of VesperTV".

Unmodified official builds may be shared as-is, under their original name.

Questions or permission requests: open an issue at https://github.com/NoXqc/VesperTV.
